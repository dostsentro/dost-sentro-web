Who we are

Building a regional scholar community rooted in knowledge, service, and leadership.

SENTRO, or the Scholars' Empowered Nationalistic and Tactical Regional Organization, is the regional organization of DOST-SEI scholars in Central Luzon.

Our network spans Aurora, Bataan, Bulacan, Nueva Ecija, Pampanga, and Tarlac, and is powered by 14 university-based Associations of DOST Scholars organizations. These ADS organizations serve as the backbone of SENTRO and provide the foundation for coordination, collaboration, and collective impact across institutions.

Together, we aim to develop scholars who are not only academically capable, but also competent, service-oriented, innovative, and future-ready leaders.

Our centerpiece

Project TRISKO: the torch that carries scholarship into action.

Project TRISKO

Project TRISKO is SENTRO’s pioneer sustainable initiative and one of our most impactful programs. Through this project, we promote the DOST Undergraduate Scholarship Program and help aspiring scholars prepare for the scholarship examination through review sessions and volunteer-led initiatives.

⚑

What the torch symbolizes

The torch represents knowledge, opportunity, leadership, and service. It reflects the light carried by scholars, officers, committee members, DOST personnel, and volunteers—showing that SENTRO’s success is built by people working together.
